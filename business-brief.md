# Business Brief

Creator:
Lenny Haze

Audience:
Small local businesses in the Biloxi Area who want more customers but are not utilizing social media properly to grow the business. This is going to grow into government agencies that need reliable social media management and prefer working with veteran-owned businesses.

Core promise:
"Real results. Fast Growth.  Veteran Reliability." I help small businesses and government agencies grow their customer base through professional social media management-- Backed by real military discipline, sales expertise, and a track record of building successful businesses. 

Video goal:
Get small business owners to hire me for social media management by showing them how it brings real customers. Include an CTA for them to comment or DM me with interest.

Video concept:
Open with a hook showing struggling businesses, spend the middle showing my simple 3-step process (post quality content + post consistently + respond to people = real customers), and close with proof of results from your first clients and your offer (first 3 businesses get a free month). Make it under 12 minutes and position me as a veteran business owner who understands their struggle, not just a social media guy.

Offer path:
The video should naturally lead from awareness to interest, which allows them to make a decision. First month free, after 30 days we either part ways after they see the results or the rate becomes $500 a month

Voice:
Direct, strategic, high-energy, premium, practical, no fluff.

Constraints:
- Do not make this a generic video about social media management.
- Video must hook them is 15 seconds.
- Show how we target their ideal customer, not everyone with our 3-step process.
- Explain that the money is not in tools. The money is in systems.
